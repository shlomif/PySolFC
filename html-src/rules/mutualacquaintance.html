Mutual Acquaintance

Numerica type. 2 decks. 1 redeal.

Quick Description

A two deck variant of Acquaintance.

Like Auld Lang Syne, but with eight rows and one redeal is allowed, in which the tableau piles are combined and placed on top of each other to form a new deck.

One set of foundations is built up from ace to king, and the other is built down from king to ace.

Notes

Autodrop is disabled for this game.
