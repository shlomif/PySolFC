Rectangular Rovers

Montana type. 1 stripped deck. No redeal.

Object

Group all the cards in sets of 5 cards in ascending sequence by suit from Ace to Five.

Rules

The aces through sixes are dealt in five rows of six cards each. The sixes are removed, creating blank spaces.

A card can be moved to an adjacent empty space, horizontally or vertically, similar to Matrix. By moving cards this way, the goal is to arrange all the cards in sequences from ace through five, in rows of the same suit.
