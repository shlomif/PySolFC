Sudoku

Sudoku is a single-player puzzle game.

Object

Arrange the pieces in the grid so every row, column, and 3x3 block contains a copy of each rank from 1 through 9.

Rules

A number of numbers are dealt to the grid. The numbers placed in the grid during the initial deal cannot be changed.

Fill in the empty spots with the numbers from 1 through 9. Your goal is to arrange the tiles so that every row, column, and 3x3 block, contains all the numbers from 1 through 9.

Notes

Every deal has one solution. Higher level deals may require more advanced reasoning techniques to solve.

Autodrop is disabled for this game.
