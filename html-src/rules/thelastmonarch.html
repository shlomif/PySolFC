The Last Monarch (Der letzte Monarch)

One-Deck game type. 1 deck. No redeal.

Object

Move 51 cards (all cards except the last King) to the foundations.

Rules

The 52 cards are dealt to four rows of thirteen cards each.

Cards on the tableau must be captured by one of their left, right, top or bottom neighbour. The captured card is then moved to the foundations, if it can be moved there - the foundations are built up by same suit from ace to king, or to the reserves if it cannot be. The capturing card moves into place.

Cards from the reserve can only be moved to the foundations.

Notes

Quickplay is disabled for this game.
