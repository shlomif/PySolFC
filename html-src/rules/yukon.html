Yukon

Yukon type. 1 deck. No redeal.

Object

Move all cards to the foundations.

Rules

Cards are dealt to seven piles in the tableau, one card in the first pile, two in the second, and so on. Only the top card of each pile is face-up. Then, the remaining cards are dealt evenly to the second through seventh pile, all face-up.

Cards in tableau are built down by alternate color. Groups of face-up cards can be moved regardless of sequence. An empty pile in the tableau can be filled with a King or a group of cards with a King on the bottom.

Foundations are built up in suit from Ace to King. Cards in foundations are no longer in play. The game is won if all cards are moved to the foundations.

History

Yukon is one of the classic solitaire card games.
